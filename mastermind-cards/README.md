# Out of the Bubble

A 24-card facilitation deck for mastermind groups of senior product managers. One person draws a card and becomes its subject for 15 minutes. The topics cover career, craft, influence and energy, so the group gets out of the usual talk about current problems.

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages. There is nothing to install or build.

1. Someone without a hot seat that day draws a card. They can veto it once, with no reason needed.
2. Press Start. The timer walks the group through the steps on the card.
3. Close with the question at the bottom of the card. The answer can become a commitment for the next meeting.

Played cards stay out of the deck until you reshuffle. This is saved only in the browser used to draw, so have one person draw and share their screen for the whole season.
