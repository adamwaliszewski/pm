Out of the bubble

One person draws a card and becomes its subject. For 15 minutes we talk about them as a senior in the profession, and current problems wait for the hot seat.

  • Someone without a hot seat today draws. The card is for them, and someone else keeps time.
  • The person drawing has one veto. No reason needed. The card goes back in the deck.
  • We talk about the person, not the company. Anyone who drifts back to a current problem hears: “save it for the hot seat”.
  • We close with the question at the bottom of the card. The answer can become a commitment. What’s said here stays in the group.
